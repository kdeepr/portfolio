I was an infrastructure analyst who realized the real job was always about the data.

The signal was always there; my job was to drag it out and put it where someone could act on it.

Now I'm finishing an M.S. in Data Science & Analytics at Grand Valley State (GPA 3.88) and going formal on what I was already doing — statistical modeling in R, ML pipelines in Python, vector search, and the data engineering plumbing underneath it all.

The thing I care most about: models that ship, results that get read, and a translation layer that doesn't break when it hits a real operator.
